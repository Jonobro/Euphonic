Import Your Spotify Playlists

×

To create custom playlists using your Spotify tracks and obtain insights into your musical preferences, you’ll first need to import some of your playlists. For the best results, include as much of your music as possible.

To get started, paste share links to your Spotify playlists below.

To obtain a playlist share link in Spotify:
  1. Navigate to the desired Spotify playlist
  2. Click the three dots (...) to open the menu
  3. Choose "Share"
  4. Click "Copy link" (or "Copy link to playlist")
  5. Paste the link into one of the fields below these instructions
Note: Your "Liked Songs" collection cannot be shared directly. To import these tracks, you'll first need to add them to a shareable playlist using the steps below (desktop only).
  1. Navigate to your "Liked Songs"
  2. Select the first track
  3. Scroll to the last track
  4. Left-click the last track while holding Shift
  5. Right-click your tracks and select "Add to playlist"
  6. Click "New playlist." The new playlist will appear at the top of your library
  7. Obtain the playlist link using the steps described above
