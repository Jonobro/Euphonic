Jonathan Yoder, operating as Euphonic Intelligence ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, share, and retain your information when you use the Euphonic Intelligence application (the "Application" or "Service").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. This Privacy Policy should be read in conjunction with our End-User License Agreement (EULA).

1. Information We Collect and Use

We collect the following types of information:

A. Chat History: The history of your conversation with the Euphonic Intelligence chatbot, including your messages and the chatbot's responses, will be stored in your session for continuity during your active use.

B. Information from Spotify: When you opt to import your Spotify music collection into Euphonic Intelligence by manually inputting the URLs of your playlists into the designated fields in the application, we make use of the "Get Playlist" endpoint of the Spotify API to retrieve the tracks from your playlists and store them securely in your session. This allows us to understand your musical preferences and create custom playlists for you using your saved songs. When you provide us with a share link for a private Spotify playlist, we use that link to become associated with the playlist in order to retrieve the song titles and artist names in the playlist.

C. Automatically Collected Technical and Usage Data (Session Data):

  • Session Identifiers: We use essential cookies to manage your session and keep it secure.
  • Track List: If you choose to import your playlists into the Application, a list of the tracks in these playlists will be cached in your session to improve performance during your active use of the Application.
  • Technical Information: Your IP address, browser type, operating system, device information, and access times may be logged and used to improve Euphonic Intelligence.
  • Usage Patterns: Pages viewed, features used, how you interact with the Application, performance metrics, and error logs may be logged and used to improve Euphonic Intelligence.

BETA NOTICE: Until otherwise stated, Euphonic Intelligence is in it's beta phase. We reserve the right to log and review conversations for quality assurance purposes during this phase.

2. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or other marketing platforms for their independent use. We share your information only in the following limited ways:

A. With Google (Gemini API):

  • To provide the core AI chat and playlist generation features of Euphonic Intelligence, we send your chat messages to Google's Gemini API for processing. If you choose to import your Spotify playlists, we also send a list of the tracks in these playlists to Gemini.
  • Google's use of this data is governed by Google's API Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  • Your Spotify data, namely the playlists you import into Euphonic Intelligence, is never retained by or ingested by Gemini as per the terms of Euphonic Intelligence's partnership with Google. When you use the application, individual stateless requests are made to Gemini's API, but this data is never stored on Gemini's servers and is never used for training purposes by Google.

B. With Spotify:

  • We do not share any of your personal information or chat messages with Spotify. We do not and cannot alter your Spotify account in any way.
  • The Spotify playlists created by Euphonic Intelligence are public and may be accessed by any Spotify user.

C. Legal Requirements:

  • We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, protect and defend our rights or property, prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the Service, protect the personal safety of users of the Service or the public, or protect against legal liability.

3. Data Retention

We retain your information as follows:

  • Chat History: Stored in your session for continuity during your active use and cleared when you press "Reset" or when your session expires (automatically occurs after two weeks).
  • Spotify Track List: Stored in your session for performance during your active use and cleared when you press "Reset" or when your session expires (automatically occurs after two weeks). This information is only stored if you choose to import your playlists into the Application.
  • Application Caching: Your data (as described in "Information We Collect and Use") may be temporarily stored in a separate, short-lived cache during your immediate use of the application. This information is never stored for more than one hour before being deleted.
  • Session Data (General): All session data (including the items described above) is cleared when you press "Reset" or when your session expires (automatically occurs after two weeks).

When you provide Euphonic Intelligence with a share link for a private Spotify playlist, we use that link to become associated with the playlist so that we can retrieve the song titles and artist names. We do not retain the playlist link nor do we save the playlist in Spotify, and we will never retrieve any playlist information after your session has ended.
